### Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: OptSolv Time Tracker has genuinely authored, category-leading product mechanics (`AutofillRadar`, `ReconstructDayDialog`, `MyTimeClient` logged-vs-calendar comparison, and anti-overwork gamification), paired with a disciplined `Sora` + `DM Sans` + `JetBrains Mono` typographic system. Where it falls back to category-interchangeable defaults is the visual execution layer of its data visualizations (stock flat Recharts bars/pies and inline tooltips), its loading transitions (flat `animate-pulse` grey rectangle slabs and generic spinning `Loader2` icons), and its surface depth (`.mesh-gradient` and `.grain-overlay` exist in `globals.css` but are unused across app cards and backdrops).

**Deterministic scan**: `impeccable detect --json src/app src/components` returned 4 findings across 3 rules (`gradient-text` ×2 in `src/app/globals.css:216` and `src/components/landing/how-it-works.tsx:275`; `ai-color-palette` ×1 in `src/components/portal/presentation/SlideTeam.tsx:15`; `side-tab` ×1 in `src/app/api/v1/docs/route.ts:151`, which is a false positive on Swagger Markdown blockquotes).
